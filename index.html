How it works

Turn consequential actions into reviewable evidence.

DAR sits alongside existing agents and workflows. It records observable actions, authority, evidence, policy checks, and human decisions without pretending to capture hidden model reasoning or replacing the original system.

1

Define authority

Record the requested action, permitted scope, prohibited actions, principal, and required approval boundaries.

2

Capture evidence

Link the action actually taken to its agent, tool, sources, policy version, human interventions, output, and receipt signature.

3

Verify and reconstruct

Let an independent reviewer confirm integrity, issuer trust, authority, approvals, missing evidence, and stated limitations.

A focused assurance primitive

More than a log. More honest than a blanket “verified.”

A DAR separates what can be cryptographically verified from what still requires human, policy, or outcome review. Missing evidence and unresolved claims remain visible instead of being collapsed into a single pass indicator.

What a receipt can include

✓Agent and principal identity
✓Authority and delegation
✓Requested versus executed action
✓Sources and evidence references
✓Policy and model version
✓Human approval or correction
✓Signature and issuer trust
✓Outcome and limitations
Evidence before claims

Case studies are how DAR earns trust.

The project is validating DAR through bounded workflows that expose what happened, where human authority mattered, which evidence exists, and which conclusions cannot yet be made.

1

Secure development

Reconstruct AI-assisted software work from authorized scope through code changes, tests, security scans, pull requests, and human-controlled publication.

2

Procurement workflow

Document opportunity research, source review, requirements, claims, drafts, missing owner information, and the hard stop before submission or binding representation.

3

External controlled pilot

Test a bounded AI-assisted workflow with an outside company using approved evidence, human review, prohibited actions, and publication only with explicit permission.

Clear boundaries

Evidence without surveillance or lock-in.

DAR is meant to make specific actions verifiable, not to become another monitoring platform or a copy of every system log.

What DAR is

  • An independent evidence and authorization layer
  • Portable assurance for consequential agent actions
  • A record of scope, evidence, controls, and human decisions
  • A way to reconstruct actions across tools and organizations
Explore the architecture

What DAR is not

  • Employee-surveillance or generic activity logging
  • The AI model or operational decision system
  • Proof that an AI answer or decision is true
  • Government approval, certification, or automatic compliance

Deliberate readiness

Build credibility in the right order.

DAR is progressing from verifiable demonstrations to external evidence and controlled pilots before making production or government-readiness claims.
