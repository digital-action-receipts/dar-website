# Enterprise and government case-study report

## Executive summary

Case Study 1 shows how an agent-assisted repository change can be bounded, tested, recorded, and independently corroborated before publication.

## Business problem and scope

The problem is traceability of agent-assisted changes and evidence without expanding into customer accounts, managed storage, production authentication, or certification. Human approval is the transition point for external publication.

## Evidence lifecycle and validation

Evidence begins with a clean-worktree snapshot, continues through local commits and repeatable tests/security checks, and ends with PR/CI/artifact references. The manifest preserves provenance and limitations.

## Audit, procurement, privacy, and retention relevance

The package provides reviewable provenance and attribution useful to due diligence. It contains synthetic data only, performs no managed retention or privacy operation, and does not replace a DPA, records schedule, incident plan, accessibility review, or control assessment.

## Recommended pilot applications

Use for low-risk synthetic documentation, engineering evidence rehearsals, and reviewer training. Do not use as evidence of regulated production operation without separate approvals and controls.
