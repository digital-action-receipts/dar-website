# Reviewer questionnaire

Score each item 0 (no evidence), 1 (partial), or 2 (strong evidence).

1. Evidence completeness: are required artifacts and references present?
2. Evidence integrity: do hashes, commits, PR, run, and artifact references agree?
3. Authorization clarity: are scope, approvals, and prohibited actions explicit?
4. Human-agent attribution: can agent actions be separated from human actions?
5. External corroboration: does GitHub independently confirm the recorded result?
6. Reproducibility: can a reviewer rerun the local checks?
7. Limitation transparency: are unsupported claims clearly excluded?

Final assessment: record each score, total /14, unresolved questions, and an overall confidence of low, medium, or high. A high confidence score is not a certification or production approval.
